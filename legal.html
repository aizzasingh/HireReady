Privacy Policy
How we collect, use, and protect your data
Last updated: January 2025
💡

We believe privacy should be simple. We only collect what we need to provide the service, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Information We Collect

When you use HireReady, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Resume content — the text extracted from uploaded PDF or DOCX files for analysis purposes.
  • Job descriptions — any job description text you paste for matching analysis.
  • Usage data — page views, feature usage, and session duration to improve the product.
  • Device information — browser type, operating system, and approximate location (country level).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the HireReady analysis service.
  • Generate ATS scores, keyword match results, and skill gap reports.
  • Train and improve our machine learning models — only using anonymized, aggregated data.
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset).
  • Respond to your support requests and feedback.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on servers located in India. We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit) for all data. Resume files are processed in isolated environments and are not shared between users.

🛡️

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or resume content with third parties for marketing purposes — ever.

4. Data Retention

We retain your resume data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data — including uploaded resumes and analysis results — is permanently deleted within 30 days.

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required to keep you logged in and maintain your session. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Export your analysis history in JSON format.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@HireReady.ai.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
